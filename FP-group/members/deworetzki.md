My research lies at the intersection of classical computer science and natural language processing (NLP).
I apply tools from program language technologies such as type systems, program analysis and optimizations to improve searching in large, richly-annotated text corpora.

Further, I am organizing the group's [weekly Functional Programming Seminar Series](https://fptalks.cse.chalmers.se/).
